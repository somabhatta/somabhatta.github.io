About

Somadutta Bhatta

I’m a postdoctoral researcher with the ALICE experiment at Utrecht University and Nikhef.

I completed my PhD at Stony Brook University, working with the ATLAS and STAR collaborations. My research uses fluctuations in particle momentum to study the collective expansion of the quark–gluon plasma and the geometry of the colliding nuclei.
